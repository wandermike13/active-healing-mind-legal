Privacy Policy: Active Healing Mind

Effective date: 8 November 2026 Operator: Anappaday (sole proprietor, Japan) Contact: wandermike@gmail.com

Active Healing Mind is a relaxation and guided-imagery app. It is not a medical device and it does not provide medical care. This policy describes exactly what the app collects, where it goes, and how to remove it. It was written by reading the app's source code, not by copying a template.

The short version

  • Your photos never leave your phone.
  • Your session records are stored on the app's servers under your own account, so you can sign in on a new phone and keep your history.
  • Your password is never stored on the device and never stored by us in readable form.
  • No advertising identifier or third-party advertising SDK is present in this version.
  • You can clear your history inside the app. Account deletion is by request to the contact address above until an in-app option ships.

What the app collects

Account. An email address and a password. The password is sent once to the sign-in endpoint over HTTPS and is not written to device storage, logs, or crash reports. The server keeps a credential hash. The app stores a session token on your device so you stay signed in.

Session records. Each completed session stores: your account identifier, the date and time, the session number, the pain rating you gave before (0 to 10), the pain rating you gave after, the body area you selected or typed, which binaural track you used, and any notes you chose to write.

Pain log entries. A matching row per session: date, pain rating, body area, notes.

Photos. If you choose to compare against your own photo, the app opens your photo library or camera. The image is used on screen only. It is never uploaded, never written to our servers, and not included in backups made by the app.

Local-only data. An in-progress session draft (so a dropped phone or a closed app does not lose your place), an onboarding flag, your disclaimer acknowledgement, and your reminder preference. These stay on your device and are removed when you clear the app's data or uninstall it.

Reminders. The Sunday reminder is a local notification scheduled by your own phone. No server sends it, so no server knows whether you opened the app.

What the app does not collect

  • No location data, at any precision.
  • No contacts, calendar, microphone, or health-platform data.
  • No advertising identifier, and no third-party advertising SDK in version 1.0.0.
  • No analytics or crash-reporting SDK in version 1.0.0.
  • No photos, and no image analysis of any kind.

If a later version adds any of these, this policy changes first and the change is declared in the store listing.

Purchases

The optional ad-free unlock is processed by the app store you installed from, through RevenueCat as the purchase-management service. RevenueCat receives your app account identifier (a random account ID, not your email), the product identifier, and the resulting entitlement state. Your card details never touch the app and are never seen by us: the store handles payment.

Removing the purchase or managing it happens in your store account settings, not in this app.

Where data is stored and who processes it

  • Application backend: Base44 (app 6a1b336279a3bc05b2f2196e), which stores account and session records. Data in transit uses HTTPS.
  • Purchases: RevenueCat, plus Google Play or the Apple App Store.
  • On the device: the local items listed above.

Records are isolated per account. One account cannot read another account's sessions. If two people share a phone, each signs in separately and each keeps a separate history.

How long data is kept

Session and pain records are kept until you delete them or ask for deletion of your account. There is no automatic expiry, because the whole point of the app is a long record you can look back on.

Your choices

  • Clear device history: Settings, then clear history. Removes the local copy and the in-progress draft.
  • Delete a record: delete the session in the History screen.
  • Delete your account and all server-side records: email the contact address above from the address on the account. Requests are actioned within 30 days. This deletes the account, its session records, and its pain logs.
  • Sign out: Settings. Signing out removes the token from the device.

Children

The app is not directed at children. It is not enrolled in any child-directed program, and it does not knowingly collect data from anyone under 13 (or the minimum age in your country).

Medical disclaimer

Active Healing Mind does not diagnose, treat, cure or prevent any condition. It is a relaxation and attention tool, not a substitute for advice from a doctor or physiotherapist. If pain is sudden, severe or worsening, or comes with numbness, weakness or loss of control, seek medical help rather than using this app.

Changes
