Delete your Active Healing Mind account

Two ways to do it. Neither one costs anything.

In the app, immediately

  1. Open Active Healing Mind.
  2. Go to Settings, then Your data.
  3. Tap Delete account and data.
  4. Type DELETE to confirm.

Your sessions, pain logs and notes are erased at that moment, and the account is closed. There is no waiting period and nothing to undo.

By email, if you cannot open the app

Write to wandermike@gmail.com from the address you signed up with, with the subject "Delete my account".

Requests are actioned within 30 days, usually much sooner. You will get a reply confirming that the account and its records are gone.

What gets deleted

  • Every session record: date, pain rating before and after, body area, breathing track used, and any notes you typed.
  • Every pain log entry.
  • The account itself, including the email address tied to it.

What is not held in the first place

  • Photographs you take or pick inside the app never leave your phone. They are not uploaded, so there is nothing for us to delete.
  • There is no advertising identifier, no location data, and no contact list access.

Purchases

If you bought the ad-free unlock, the purchase record belongs to Google Play, not to this account. Deleting the account does not refund it, and reinstalling lets you restore it with Restore Purchases.
